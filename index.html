Flagship Capability

Nitora Federal

Nitora Federal is a cross-program recall intelligence, exposure-resolution, and recall-effectiveness evidence layer designed to help authorized teams understand where recalled products may remain in commerce, reconcile multiple recall records, prioritize follow-up, and preserve the evidence behind those decisions.

Nitora Federal is designed to complement—not replace—source systems and organizational authority. Regulatory, compliance, exposure, and recall-effectiveness determinations remain with authorized personnel.

Current work focuses on controlled evaluation, deterministic evidence handling, source provenance, measurable workflow performance, and independent review.
