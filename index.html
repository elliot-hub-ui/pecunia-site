Flagship Capability

Nitora Federal

Nitora Federal is a cross-program recall intelligence, exposure-resolution, and recall-effectiveness evidence layer designed to help authorized teams understand where recalled products may remain in commerce, reconcile multiple recall records, prioritize follow-up, and preserve the evidence behind those decisions.

It is designed to complement — not replace — source systems and organizational authority.

Reconciles recall records while retaining source history.
Uses deterministic matching and explicit machine states.
Organizes exposure, effectiveness, follow-up, and evidence workflows.
Preserves audit history and content-addressed evidence.
Leaves regulatory and compliance determinations with authorized personnel.
